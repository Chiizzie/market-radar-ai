# Remaining evidence gates for live research calls

3 November 2026. Connecting news to a dashboard does not clear these gates.

| Gate | Existing evidence | What remains |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instrument identity | Trading 212 catalogue metadata | Account-specific permission/availability; crypto eligibility separately |
| Executable price | Alpaca IEX context, Yahoo/Kraken research candles | Relevant broker bid/ask, freshness, market coverage and spread |
| Costs and EUR sizing | EUR draft and FX scenarios | Verified account fees/FX treatment, slippage allowance, realistic available allocation |
| Catalyst relevance | Bounded company and public publisher leads | Entity relevance, independent source support, material event time and missing coverage |
| Session and expiry | Calendar, Dublin cutoff and expiry checks | Recheck at each candidate decision and any eventual execution |
| Decision | Eight-stage review pipeline tested | Complete fresh evidence packet; remaining blockers must remain visible |

The scanner news collector covers NVDA/MSFT/AAPL query leads and Fed/Microsoft/
NVIDIA publisher feeds. It does not provide complete market or crypto news coverage,
an earnings calendar, or real-time successful-trader transactions. Public investor
and insider disclosures must retain publication and transaction dates if later added.

The current NVDA paper draft still uses an assumed 40 bps round-trip cost and EUR500
fictional allocation. News delivery must not silently replace those assumptions or
turn a completed but unapproved review into trade authorization.
