# Persistent opportunities and private holding reviews

The Opportunity page used only a scanner capture and had no supplied plan. Approved entry windows also expire independently of an actual broker holding. This change reads stored approved archives and reconciled private Invest holdings so an open position keeps its original trigger, stop, target and exit deadline after the entry window closes.

Actual holding reviews are a separate read-only policy (`position-monitor-v1`), not changes to paper fills, paper outcomes, AI verdicts or order permissions. Current holding episodes are reconciled from complete broker fill history, including partial sells and adds; corporate actions, quantity discrepancies and incomplete histories remain unlinked. A call must have been approved and within its entry window when the holding opened. Later calls cannot replace it. The original native USD levels use the plan's captured FX.

Each scheduled account sync fetches completed IEX 5-minute bars specifically for linked holdings, independently of the broad discovery selection. Two consecutive post-entry closes below the trigger prompt a failed-breakout review; two closes below their rolling 20-bar volume-weighted typical prices flag weakening. Completed closes through the original stop/target and reminders within ten minutes of, and after, the original exit deadline are also reported. Insufficient, gapped, mismatched or stale bars cannot produce a healthy status. These new review rules are transparent heuristics, not demonstrated profitable exits. They do not continuously re-evaluate news and do not replace protective broker orders.

Only the existing signed private account payload contains fills, holdings and review records. The private owner/readers RLS remains unchanged. Telegram uses the existing private bot/channel, with at-most-once delivery per holding episode/reason/day; public delivery records contain only HMAC-derived opaque identifiers and reserved/confirmed flags. Ambiguous sends are never retried. No account payloads or financial summaries are logged, cached or uploaded to public workflow artifacts. No AI budget change or paid AI invocation is added.

Opportunity now sits in the primary navigation; Journal is under More. Active watches, actual open holdings and past calls are distinguished. Invalidated holdings remain prominently visible until the broker reports them closed. Missing account data is not interpreted as closure. Stale cached holdings are labelled; protected data clears on auth changes. Archived plans retain their original expiry and never become fresh entry instructions.

Validation: focused deterministic tests cover expiry retention, broker closure, wrong/later call association, partial exits and adds, bad candle data, time exits without market data, deduplication and ambiguous Telegram delivery, private schema identity/time checks, and USD plan/chart display. A temporary private cross-language check linked both current holdings to their original archived calls; no private fixture is committed. Production frontend build and scoped lint are checked before deployment.

Sources for data semantics: https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stockbars and https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/v1.1/docs/historical-stock-data-1 . IEX is a single-exchange reference feed, not the broker execution quote.
