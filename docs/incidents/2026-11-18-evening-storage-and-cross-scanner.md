All times are Europe/Dublin. The scanner recorded five eligible stock candidates at 19:20. Its dashboard POST returned an ambiguous error and the collection job sent a failure notice. The run preserved the market and qualification files. Its old delivery code discarded the request fingerprint and underlying HTTP or transport reason. The dependent paper review skipped the capture. Five eligible candidates means five candidates passed initial screening. The review had issued no trade verdict. [19:20 run](https://github.com/Chiizzie/market-radar-ai/actions/runs/44379424183)

The 19:15 run had already stored its snapshot, then timed out reading durable state for review. Other workflows reported storage timeouts and an independent database check returned HTTP 504. The database later answered at 19:36. A bounded read then found the 19:16 snapshot, and a 19:26 capture that reached storage at 19:29:17. It returned no row generated during the 19:20 scan. The records establish intermittent service and late delivery. The hosting cause is unknown. Execution research previously fetched 155,248 immutable events over 166 storage pages. The 19:15 manual report also fetched its comparison history. Both jobs now restore authenticated watcher prefixes and locally revalidate all restored events. Each partition still requires a successful authoritative tail read. Empty, missing, invalid and expired caches use the existing signed read path. The execution job saves shared past-day prefixes once per UTC day. Replaying old data consumes local CPU and disk even with a cache hit. Cold startup still requires the initial authoritative history.

## 20:00–20:30 Dublin storage incident

The 20:00 and 20:05 scans each collected 80 current stocks and 30 current crypto instruments. Both timed out waiting for snapshot publication. The 20:20 run restored its comparison and entry checkpoints, then both workers exhausted their signed storage reads. The collector timed out waiting for its snapshot acknowledgement and the paper review was skipped. [20:00 scan](https://github.com/Chiizzie/market-radar-ai/actions/runs/44386432235) · [20:05 scan](https://github.com/Chiizzie/market-radar-ai/actions/runs/44387256643) · [20:20 scan](https://github.com/Chiizzie/market-radar-ai/actions/runs/44389948591) · [Incident 25](https://github.com/Chiizzie/market-radar-ai/issues/25)

PostgreSQL logged statement timeouts around 20:12 and 20:19. Two advisory lock keys matched the database's own hash of the operational health and cross-scanner audit experiment IDs for 18 November. Waits on those keys ranged from 2.46 to 4.95 seconds. A sample at 20:15 showed 17 connections against a limit of 60. Both reports normally retain a full copy in GitHub Actions. Their earlier clients could send the same report three times after an acknowledgement timeout. These waits establish contention on those two reports. The cause of the initial slowdown remains open.

An offline replay rebuilt both exact request fingerprints from the saved capture, news and original generation time. The earlier dashboard files differed from the actual requests by fractions of a second. The collector now atomically archives the exact HTTP body before the single send. Its digest can be compared with the receipt even after a timeout. The collector still reports the unconfirmed delivery and blocks the dependent review. A secondary comparison still records its full local audit but defers its remote publication when the same capture's snapshot acknowledgement is already unconfirmed. Each operational health report makes one remote write attempt and retains the existing GitHub artifact. Backup receipts and authoritative research evidence retain their retry policy.

Recovery requires an ordinary run with a timely matching snapshot receipt and a successful authoritative storage read. A local archive or a late write alone cannot establish recovery.

The 20:40 scheduled scan verified the new capture behaviour in production. Its archived HTTP body matched receipt `aa8fd5f2eb82931fc81959f761f40afee636050c528e1c83a669d348b30d8056` exactly, all 110 captured instruments were current, and the secondary comparison was saved without a second storage write. The primary acknowledgement still timed out. An independent read-only check reached the public site in 1.4 seconds. Both authenticated storage reads exhausted their attempts. The health job made one write attempt and uploaded its full report. ## Recovery through the US close

All 24 scheduled dispatches from 19:00 through 20:55 Dublin had a capture artifact. Each ZIP matched its GitHub SHA-256 digest. Four captures had a confirmed client receipt. Fourteen had an unconfirmed receipt. For ten of those, rendering the original capture and news at the receipt's generation time reproduced the fingerprint exactly. Four already contained the exact outbound body. At 21:08:57 Dublin, a read-only PostgreSQL lookup by the 14 fingerprints found the 20:00 snapshot after expiry and no matching row for the other 13. Six earlier captures had no client receipt. Their local dashboard exports retain their own timestamps and hashes. The earlier time-based observation at 19:26 is recorded above. [Run-by-run reconciliation](evidence/2026-11-18-evening-capture-reconciliation.json)

The 20 captures with missing or unconfirmed receipts contained 137 distinct paper-review packets covering 87 candidate IDs. All 137 draft entry windows lasted 180 seconds from packet assembly. After 60 seconds each still had time left. At 180 and 300 seconds every draft had expired. Actual notification times, refreshed final plans, fills and exits are separate measurements. These interrupted drafts supply research cases and establish no trading return.

The existing 90-day evidence-bank artifact now retains verified outbound bodies under `recovery_snapshot`, separately identified local display exports under `recovery_export`, and the original packet contents under `recovery_packet`. Per-artifact `capture_delivery` records link those objects to the run. Previously indexed failed runs can acquire these additional records without rewriting their original candidate histories. Candidate capture intake now runs before portfolio retrieval. A missing execution source still blocks performance evaluation. Repeating the 24-run replay read zero additional ZIPs and preserved every record.
