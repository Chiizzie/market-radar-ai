# Fresh breakout after a delayed response

The primary manual portfolio evaluates a stock watch against its original expiry. In the 16–18 November development data, all 39 acknowledged watches had expired by three minutes. This secondary paper experiment tests a new confirmation for a trader responding after three or five minutes. Both alternatives run on the same authenticated Telegram acknowledgements and quote sequence.

The control keeps the frozen manual plan. Where the original plan still has time, the alternative follows it too. For an expired plan, the alternative has up to 60 seconds from the scheduled response. The bid must first trade below the original trigger and then cross it on a distinct quote within 15 seconds. The confirming ask must be within 20 basis points of the trigger. A subsequent fresh quote is required for any fill. The original stop, target, hard exit, EUR conversion costs, shared capital and daily risk checks apply. A recorded stop or target touch ends the attempt. Gaps over 60 seconds leave coverage unknown. The reported quotes are venue reference observations.

The two delays use separate €1,000 paper portfolios. Each arm starts again for prospective watches acknowledged from 19 November in Dublin. Full cases and outcomes appear under `manual.fresh_breakout` in the existing performance artifact. The report records missing coverage, reasons for blocked entries and closed net EUR results. The experiment uses no paid models or trading credentials.

Replaying all 79,888 original manual source events at the 19:13:40 Dublin cutoff on 18 November reproduced the nine published baseline ledgers. HUN met the new confirmation at three and five minutes. CSGP also met it at five minutes. All three attempts failed the existing two-to-one reward-to-risk check after assumed costs. Neither arm recorded a fill at those delays.
