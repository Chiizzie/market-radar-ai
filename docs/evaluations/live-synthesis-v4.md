# Final research synthesis v4

## Problem

The completed v3 live trial returned a conclusion rationale identical to its debate discussion. All numeric and objection-coverage checks passed, but a repeated paragraph does not demonstrate independent synthesis.

## Change

The conclusion now contains a shorter rationale, up to three decisive concern IDs, a counterargument, and one to three concrete next-evidence requests. Decisive IDs must come from the original register without duplication; when concerns exist, at least one must be selected. An empty register does not force fabricated objections. The complete concern and missing-data acknowledgement lists remain mandatory.

The rationale cannot exactly copy the debate after case, punctuation and whitespace normalization. Counterargument cannot repeat the rationale, and next-evidence items cannot duplicate each other under the same normalization. No price, time, cost, eligibility or research-only safeguard was relaxed. The schema and instruction versions are v4; historical v3 reports remain historical records and are not rewritten.

## Verification and limits

342 local tests passed. New offline tests inject copy/paste, case/punctuation copies, unknown/duplicate/omitted decisive IDs, repeated counterarguments and repeated evidence requests. Rejections preserve cost and diagnostic drafts without promoting a final decision. A synthetic disagreement scenario preserves both continuation and failure hypotheses and the devil's advocate objection through the final review. The live workflow preflight now includes these tests.

These tests use synthetic responses and make no live AI calls. Structural acceptance is not semantic validation: a paraphrased copy or weak counterargument can still pass. The contract does not force disagreement where specialists agree. A new paid v4 trial has not been performed; paid research remains disabled. The successful v3 trial remains the latest live evidence.
