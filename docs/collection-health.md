# Collection health and review pauses

A valid empty symbol series is a data gap rather than a provider connection failure when its market also returned nonempty series. Provider errors and entirely empty unexpected market captures still fail. Collection reports expose unusable_or_stale_symbols and delivered_with_data_gaps; stale or gapped evidence never becomes eligible merely because the workflow can finish.

SCREEN_DISCOVERY_DATA=true restricts discovered leads to USD STOCK catalogue candidates with an exact Trading 212 _US_EQ ticker candidate before checking up to 24 names for clean candles during regular clock hours. Up to eight passing names receive deep evidence collection. Candidate catalogue membership is not account buy permission. If screening yields no names, the original shortlist is explicitly flagged as fallback, never claimed verified. Premarket remains context-only, without a current-session candle requirement.

Quota/budget pauses now send a non-AI STATUS Telegram notice once per Dublin day and reason. It says no review was generated and does not assert collection success. Durable reservation precedes sending; uncertain sends are never repeated. Paid review spending limits and trade admission gates are unchanged.
